# Buy Extra Ammo Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Let players buy extra reserve ammunition during the buy phase by clicking owned weapon buttons, with per-weapon floor (free auto-refill at round start) and per-weapon cap (hard ceiling).

**Architecture:** Replace the per-weapon `reserveAmmo` field with `reserveFloor` and `reserveCap`. Reuse existing weapon buttons (like the armor button cycles through states) — owned weapons with reserve below cap become "+1 mag for $50" buttons. The `resetForRound` function becomes a floor-if-below check rather than a full refill. No new menu rows, no new keybinds.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla JS (IIFE pattern, modules attach to `window.GAME`), Three.js r160.1 (global `THREE`), Vitest for tests, jsdom for test environment. No ES module imports.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-04-26-buy-ammo-design.md`
